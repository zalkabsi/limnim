## Judging criteria

The official rules describe Stage One as a pass/fail check for theme fit and use of the Learn Skill Pack, evidenced by planning documents. Stage Two has four equally weighted criteria: **Design**, **Potential Impact**, **Innovation/Idea**, and **Presentation**. The overview page displays three categories; under the rules' precedence clause, the official rules control. Technical implementation is important evidence for the working-project requirement, but it is not a separate Stage Two score in the rules.

| Criterion | Evidence in this project | Gap or highest-value action |
|---|---|---|
| Design | One-screen front-desk workflow, clear sample/live separation, responsive layout, keyboard timeline, evidence list, explicit uncertainty, and copyable guest response. | The response and strict-after boundary are browser-tested; the final video must present them clearly. |
| Potential Impact | Concrete staff task: explaining cancellation consequences with a prepared, source-linked estimate. | Keep claims as estimates and avoid labor-savings or adoption claims without evidence. |
| Innovation/Idea | Evidence-backed extraction, source quote validation, boundary semantics, and deterministic calculations and reply. | Explain this separation and the direct front-desk use in the pitch. |
| Presentation | Limnim-branded public source, new screenshot and thumbnail, draft description, and a local sample-mode demo draft are ready. | Verify a live extraction, finish and publish the final video, and save Devpost fields. |

## Deployment and scope

The official rules require a project that installs and runs consistently and a public source repository plus video. They do not require a separate public website deployment. A free Render sample-only service is currently reachable at <https://limnim-sample-demo.onrender.com>; the Tokyo sample opens directly and `/api/analyze` returns the expected `sample_demo_only` response. No Render settings or credentials were changed during this audit. If live analysis is ever enabled publicly, the unauthenticated endpoint needs usage controls first.
