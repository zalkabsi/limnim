## Project name

RefundCurve

## Elevator pitch

See how each hotel cancellation deadline changes your potential refund, with every estimate tied to the policy text.

## Project description

RefundCurve turns a hotel's cancellation policy into a timeline of potential refunds and penalties. Paste the policy, enter the booking total, check-in details, and property's time zone, then explore supported cancellation deadlines and see how the estimate changes. Every supported rule links back to its policy excerpt.

AI helps extract the terms; RefundCurve checks that each result matches its evidence and calculates amounts deterministically. If a deadline is unclear, rules conflict, or a fee is outside the demo's supported terms, the result stays unresolved instead of guessing. Travelers can see what's at stake before deciding whether to cancel. RefundCurve does not contact hotels, cancel bookings, guarantee refunds, or determine legal rights.

It's for travelers who want a clearer picture before deciding whether to cancel now or wait.

## What I learned — personal draft

The biggest lesson for me was that an extracted quote isn't enough on its own. I had to check that the date, time, penalty, and booking scope all match the evidence, then keep the math separate from the AI. When a policy doesn't say enough, showing the uncertainty is more useful than filling in the gaps.
