## Project description

RefundCurve helps travelers understand how a hotel's cancellation terms could affect the money they get back. Paste in the policy, add the booking total and the property's time zone, then explore the supported deadlines on an interactive refund timeline. Move the cancellation time to see the estimated refund and penalty change, and check the policy excerpt behind each rule.

AI helps read the policy; the app checks the evidence and does the financial calculation. If a deadline is unclear, rules conflict, or a fee is outside what the demo supports, RefundCurve explains the gap instead of guessing. It does not contact a hotel, cancel a booking, guarantee a refund, or determine legal rights.

It's for travelers who want a clearer picture before deciding whether to cancel now or wait.

## What I learned — personal draft

The biggest lesson for me was that an extracted quote isn't enough on its own. I had to check that the date, time, penalty, and booking scope all match the evidence, then keep the math separate from the AI. When a policy doesn't say enough, showing the uncertainty is more useful than filling in the gaps.
